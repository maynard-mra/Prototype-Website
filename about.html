About Us

A charitable organisation on a mission to make cybersecurity accessible to everyone, not just IT professionals.

Our Story

How KnowYourCyber Began

KnowYourCyber was founded in 2019 by a group of cybersecurity professionals who noticed a troubling gap: while technical knowledge about cyber threats was abundant in specialist communities, it rarely reached the everyday people who needed it most.
Elderly relatives were falling for phishing scams. Small business owners were losing thousands to ransomware. Students were having their accounts hijacked. The knowledge to prevent these attacks existed, it just wasn't reaching the right people, in the right language. We built KnowYourCyber to fix that.

What we believe

Our Values

Accessibility

Cybersecurity knowledge should never be locked behind jargon or expensive training. Everything we produce is free, clear, and designed for people without a technical background.

Transparancy

We are open about our sources, our methodology, and our limitations. If a piece of information is uncertain, we say so. Trust is built through honesty, not confidence.

Practical

We focus on actionable advice that makes a real difference. Understanding cryptography theory is less useful to most people than knowing how to spot a phishing email.

Community-Driven

Our content is shaped by the questions real people ask. We listen to our community, respond to emerging threats, and update our guidance to reflect the current threat landscape.
