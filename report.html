Before you Report

What to Include

As much detail as possible

Dates, times, sender names, email addresses, URLs, and a description of what happened all help our team verify and investigate the threat.

Screenshots (if possible)

If you have a screenshot of the phishing email or suspicious page, mention it in the description. We may follow up to request it.

Do not click suspicious links

Copy-paste URLs rather than clicking them. Never download attachments from suspicious sources before reporting.

In immediate danger?

If a crime is in progress or you've been a victim of fraud, contact your local law enforcement directly. This form is for reporting and awareness only.
