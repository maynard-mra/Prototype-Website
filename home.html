Who we are

Our Identity

KnowYourCyber is a charitable organization dedicated to increasing public awareness of cyber threats. In today’s digital world, cyber risks are constantly evolving, affecting individuals from all walks of life. We work to make cybersecurity knowledge simple, accessible, and practical, helping people understand the dangers they may face online and how to recognize them early.

What we do

Our Mission

We aim to help individuals protect themselves against cyber-attacks and respond effectively in emergencies. By promoting safe online practices and providing clear guidance, we empower people to secure their personal information, reduce risks, and take the right actions if they become victims of cybercrime.

Has Your Data Been Exposed?

Over the years we have catalogued thousands of data breaches affecting hundreds of millions of people worldwide. Search our database to check whether your email address, username, or other personal information appeared in a known data leak.
